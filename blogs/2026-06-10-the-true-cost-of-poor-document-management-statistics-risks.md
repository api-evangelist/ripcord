In today's digital-first business environment, information is one of an organization's most valuable assets. Yet many businesses still rely on paper files, disconnected repositories, legacy document management systems, or unstructured data scattered across departments. This often leads to situations where employees waste time searching for information, compliance risks increase, customer service slows down, and organizations struggle to take advantage of business process automation, intelligent document processing, and AI initiatives.
